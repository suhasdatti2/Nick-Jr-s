Our Story

Two traditions, one family, and a quarter-century of feeding the neighborhood with care.

How it began

From a Corner Grill to a Neighborhood Favorite

It started in 1998 when Nick Sr. opened a small corner grill with a single flat-top and his mother's gyro recipe brought over from Thessaloniki. He had one rule: never cut a corner you'd notice on the plate.

Today his son, Nick Jr., carries that same rule forward. We still press every patty by hand, still stack our gyro spit each morning, and still make the tzatziki and Nick Sauce from scratch — because that's the way it was meant to be done.
