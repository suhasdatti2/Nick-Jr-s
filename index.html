Our story

A neighborhood tradition.

It began in 1998 when Nick Sr. opened a corner grill with a single flat-top and his mother's gyro recipe carried over from Thessaloniki — with one rule: never cut a corner you'd taste on the plate.

Today his son carries that rule forward. We still press every patty by hand, stack the gyro spit each morning, and make the tzatziki and Nick Sauce from scratch. Fresh-ground beef, produce delivered daily, and bread baked locally — never frozen, never rushed.

Pull up to the counter, gather the table for a birthday, or grab a bag to go. You'll leave already planning your next visit.
