Our Story

Two traditions, one family kitchen.

It began in 1998 when Nick Sr. opened a corner grill with a single flat-top and his mother's gyro recipe carried over from Thessaloniki. One rule guided everything: never cut a corner you'd taste on the plate.

Today his son carries that rule forward. We still press every patty by hand, stack the gyro spit each morning, and make the tzatziki and Nick Sauce from scratch — because that's how it's meant to be done.

— The Nick Jr's Family
