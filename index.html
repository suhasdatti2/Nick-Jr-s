Our story

An Everett original.

Imaginative burgers, gyros & sides, prepared fresh in a colorful, counter-serve kitchen on Evergreen Way — with sunny outdoor seating and a line that says it all.

From overstuffed gyros to smashed-to-order burgers and loaded sides, everything is made to order. Pull up to the counter, grab a bag to go, or take a seat out on the patio when the sun's out.

More than two thousand reviews and a 4.7-star average later, we're still grilling like the whole neighborhood is watching — because it is.

What sets us apart

Two kitchens, one obsession with flavor.

From the American grill to the Mediterranean spit, a few things never change.

Flame-Grilled

Hand-pressed patties seared on a screaming-hot flat-top — crisp edges, made to order.

Hand-Carved Gyros

Seasoned meat slow-roasted on a vertical spit and carved fresh into warm pita.

Loaded Sides

Hand-cut fries, gyro fries, onion rings and hand-spun shakes — the full lineup.

Counter & Patio

Quick, friendly counter service with colorful outdoor seating when the sun's out.
